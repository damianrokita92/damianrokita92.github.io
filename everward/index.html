The story

From a nobody to a hero

A boy wakes up beside a campfire in a quiet village, with no parents, no title and no past he can remember. An old man by the fire is the first to greet him.

From here on it is up to you: train, fight, earn the trust of the people around you and slowly turn a nobody into someone the whole village can count on.

Everward is a 2D action RPG with real-time combat, a world full of monsters and a hero you shape the way you want.

Features

What awaits you

Combat

  • Real-time melee with swords, axes, maces and more
  • Block with a shield or parry at the right moment
  • Bows and crossbows with headshots
  • Dozens of monsters and boss fights, up to a young dragon breathing fire

Magic and abilities

  • Skill trees with active abilities, auras and passive talents
  • Nine elements of magic with 108 spells
  • Elemental damage and resistances
  • Potions, poison and antidotes

A world to explore

  • Meadows, woods, caves, the glowing Crystal Depths and an old Dungeon
  • The forest settlement of Woodhaven and the Mage Tower
  • A hidden Dragon Burrow, the Foothills and Greypeak
  • Day and night pass as you fight, rest and sleep

Your village

  • Defend the village from monster raids alongside its guards
  • Help the villagers with quests and earn their respect
  • Chop wood, mine ore, smelt ingots, fish, forage and cook

Knightly battles

  • Duel 20 knights in the Knightly League
  • Lead your allies in skirmishes against rival kingdoms
  • Build barricades, charge together and destroy the enemy standard

Grow your hero

  • Weapons, armor, shields and jewelry in several grades
  • Orbs to socket into gear and fuse at the blacksmith
  • Smithing, Alchemy, Cooking and more crafting skills
  • Classes, stat points and skills that grow with use
